## 📝 v0.0.25 Changelog

### 🏞️ Environment Interaction & Terrain Flattening Engine
* **Smooth-to-Blocky Terrain Flattening**:
  - Placing any block automatically carves out overlapping Smooth Terrain (`Workspace.Terrain:FillBlock(cframe, size, Enum.Material.Air)`).
  - Smooth, rounded hills under placed structures are automatically leveled and converted into clean, stepped 2-stud blocky voxel foundations (`GrassBlock1x1` / `DirtBlock1x1`).

### 🎨 Expanded Environmental Block Textures
* **Grass Top & Dirt Sides Block (`GrassTopBlock1x1`)**:
  - Classic Minecraft-style top grass surface with warm caramel dirt sides (`BlockVisuals.luau` multi-face texture styling).
* **Cobblestone Block (`CobblestoneBlock1x1`)**:
  - Heavy chiseled cobblestone masonry (`Enum.Material.Cobblestone`).
* **Mossy Stone Block (`MossyStoneBlock1x1`)**:
  - Aged mossy ruin stone block (`Enum.Material.Pavement` with pastel green moss tint).
* **Sandstone Block (`SandstoneBlock1x1`)**:
  - Smooth desert sandstone (`Enum.Material.Sandstone`).
