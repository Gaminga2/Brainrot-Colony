# 🏰 PROJECT BRAINROT CASTLE (v0.0.1)
> **Elevator Pitch**: *Castle Story* meets Brainrot meme culture. Command voxel worker minions, harvest meme-infused resources, erect impenetrable castles, set up deadly defenses, and conquer enemy fortresses or survive endless night waves.

---

## 📜 1. Core Game Overview

* **Genre**: Real-Time Strategy (RTS) / Voxel Castle Builder & Tower Defense / PvP Siege
* **Platform**: Roblox (PC, Console, Mobile)
* **Tone & Aesthetic**: Vibrant, satirical, chaotic brainrot humor paired with crisp voxel building, responsive UI, and satisfying ragdoll combat mechanics.
* **Fair Monetization Commitment**: 0% Pay-to-Win damage/HP stats. 100% Cosmetic Drip (Skins, Soundboards, Dances, Trails) + Non-competitive Quality of Life (Blueprints, UI themes, Auto-Stash QoL).

### Mode A: Survival / Longest Run ("Endless Gyatt Defense")
* **Objective**: Protect your kingdom's **Sigma Crystal Core** for as many days/nights as possible against endless corrupted monster hordes.
* **Loop**:
  1. **Day Phase (Gather & Build)**: Assign minions to chop *Ohio Wood*, mine *Gyatt Granite*, and forge *Rizzite Alloy*. Build walls, archer posts, oil cauldrons, spike pits, and ballista towers.
  2. **Night Phase (Defense & Tactics)**: Command defensive minion squads, repair breach points live, trigger traps, and defeat night bosses.
  3. **Endless Scaling**: Every night brings stronger waves, mutant minibosses, flying gliders, and siege units.
  4. **Leaderboards**: Global & Friends Leaderboard for "Longest Days Survived", "Total Kills", and "Base Score".

### Mode B: PvP Siege ("Siege of the Sigmas")
* **Objective**: Destroy rival players' core before they destroy yours.
* **Match Phases**:
  1. **Peace / Prep Phase (3-7 Mins)**: Both teams gather resources on their side of the map and build custom fortified castles & siege installations.
  2. **War Phase**: Dynamic bridges/barriers drop. Command offensive siege squads (Minion Catapults, Battering Rams, Kamikaze Bombers, Glider Troops) while managing home base defense.
  3. **Overtime Sudden Death**: Cataclysmic meteor rain / collapsing borders to ensure action-packed final matches.

### A. Brainrot Minion Skins & Outfits
* **Iconic Character Skins**: Transform default minions into legendary avatars (e.g., *Gigachad Mason*, *Grimace Lumberjack*, *Mewing Knight*, *Npc Streamer*, *Subway Surfer Runner*).
* **Hat & Accessory Bundles**: Golden Crowns, Anime Hair, Diamond Chain, RGB Glasses, Capes.
* **Custom Spawn Effects**: Minions drop into battle via meteors, rainbow portals, or flying luxury cars.

### B. Castle Block & Architecture Themes
* **Wall Textures & Materials**: Neon Cyberpunk, Ancient Egyptian Gold, Rainbow Glitch, Dark Obsidian Lava, Comic Book Cel-shaded.
* **Flag & Crest Designer**: Custom images, clan logos, and animated banner flags flying above towers.

### C. Audio & Visual Flexes
* **Victory Dances**: Minions hit the *Orange Justice*, *Default Dance*, *Breakdance*, or *Mewing Pose* when winning a wave or match.
* **Soundboards & SFX Packs**: Custom sound effects for mining, wood chopping, wall placement, and combat hits.
* **Kill/Destruction Trails**: Enemy wall blocks explode into confetti, memes, or dollar bills when destroyed.

### D. Quality of Life (QoL) Purchases (Strictly Non-P2W)
* **Saved Castle Blueprints**: Store up to 10-20 pre-built castle templates to auto-ghost build in matches.
* **Advanced UI Themes**: Customized HUD colors, retro arcade frames, holographic minimaps.
* **Cosmetic Pet Followers**: Floating mini-pet companions accompanying your player avatar.
* **Auto-Sort Inventory Stash**: QoL button to instantly organize resource chests.
